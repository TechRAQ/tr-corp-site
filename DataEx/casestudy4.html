Products Mapping – Printers and Cartridges

Implemented DataEx for online UK retailer to help them with maintaining and listing compatible cartridges for all the printers they sell.

  • When a user inputs the Printer Company & Model, compatible cartridges are displayed for purchase.

  • When a new printer is introduced by the OEM but not listed in the dealer’s database, DataEx is run regularly & fetches the Printer & Catridge details from OEM sites and push to Retailer websites.

  • Any updates to existing Printer OR Cartridges will be fetched and loaded to retailed database database.
