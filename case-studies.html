DomainUS auto finance / lending operations
FocusAI document processing
ValueFaster review, stronger auditability, better exception handling

AI-powered document intake and data extraction for loan operations

A US auto finance business needed a modern way to process loan documents arriving from multiple sources, including individual documents and combined deal jackets with varied state, dealer, vendor, scan quality, and layout differences.

  • Document classification and deal jacket splitting
  • OCR/LLM extraction, field normalization, and business-rule validation
  • Confidence indicators, manual review workflows, audit history, and structured output
